Harlequinade is a very rare ballet but its variations are frequently seen in ballet competitions. It's terribly age inappropriate but too often danced by young (and talented) ballerinas.

###### See [dancetabs](https://dancetabs.com/2018/06/american-ballet-theatre-harlequinade-premiere-new-york/) for a very good review "*American Ballet Theatre – Harlequinade (premiere) – New York*")

Nevertheless, here's a version by Crystal Huang — her footwork and musicality are impeccable. She portraits a graceful, adorable Columbine. I would generally hope for Columbine to be more flirtful. But Crystal's version is near perfect: and I think for now the best version on the internet:
